The physicians, leaders, and volunteers who make JWPA's mission real across New Jersey. Hover over any card to learn more.

Founder & President
SN
Founder & President
Dr. Sudha Nahar, MD
Gastroenterologist · Allied Digestive Health · Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital

"I founded JWPA because I wanted every woman physician to have a community where she belongs — and every young person with a dream of medicine to have someone who looks like them show them the way."

Dr. Sudha Nahar is a practicing gastroenterologist, a physician leader, and the founder of the Jersey Women Physician Alliance. What began in 2008 as informal gatherings of women physicians grew into a formal nonprofit organization in 2018, now counting 600+ members across New Jersey. She also founded Teens With Stethoscopes (TWS), JWPA's flagship student immersion program, which hosted its inaugural event on March 21, 2026 at RWJUH.
